# CONTOUR — WEBSITE REVAMP MASTER BRIEF

Redesign the existing CONTOUR website completely while retaining the brand name CONTOUR.

The website must feel like a sophisticated, contemporary, premium fashion brand—not a generic plus-size ecommerce store.

## BRAND CONCEPT

CONTOUR is an India-first D2C fashion brand focused exclusively on dresses.

The brand is built around the concept of contouring and sculpting through intelligent clothing design. The primary proposition is NOT “plus-size fashion.” The primary proposition is:

**DRESSES DESIGNED AROUND YOUR PROPORTIONS.**

Contour uses silhouette, proportion, structure, drape, seam placement, ease and construction to create dresses that create a considered, sculpted appearance while remaining comfortable and wearable.

The initial collection is available in:

**L, XL, 2XL, 3XL and 4XL**

Do not describe Contour primarily as a “plus-size brand.” The brand is a sculpting-focused fashion brand that initially serves an extended-size customer through L–4XL.

## BRAND POSITIONING

Primary brand line:

**SCULPTED BY DESIGN.**

Supporting proposition:

**Dresses designed around your proportions.**

Core philosophy:

**Not sized up. Thought through.**

The brand should communicate sophistication, confidence, intelligent design and timeless fashion.

Do not use body-shaming language.

Do not describe body areas as flaws.

Do not use “hide your fat,” “hide your tummy,” “fix your body,” or similar language.

Use language such as:

* designed around
* considered fit
* thoughtful coverage
* comfortable ease

* proportion
* structure
* drape
* sculpted silhouette

## TARGET CUSTOMER

Women aged approximately 25–45 in India.

She wants clothing that looks sophisticated, polished, feminine, elegant and expensive.

She may have difficulty finding dresses that fit properly around the arms, bust, tummy, waist, hips or thighs.

She does not necessarily identify primarily as a “plus-size shopper.”

She wants beautiful fashion first and intelligent fit second.
